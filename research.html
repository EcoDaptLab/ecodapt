Our Research

Driving innovation in vegetation–climate science and Earth observation

Research Themes

Climate–Vegetation Modeling

We develop dynamic models that quantify how vegetation systems respond to climate variability using Earth observation and in-situ data.

Phenology & Drought Monitoring

Monitoring plant seasonal patterns to detect stress signals and support early drought warning systems across African drylands.

UAV–Satellite Calibration

Using drone data to validate and fine-tune remote sensing models for more accurate vegetation and land health assessments.

Species Distribution & Conservation

Modeling species habitat preferences to guide ex situ conservation planning, especially for understory and threatened taxa.

Land Degradation Assessment

Integrating EO indicators with machine learning to map soil degradation driven by invasive species and unsustainable land use.

Watershed & Basin Dynamics

Analyzing hydrological systems to support ecosystem-based water management and climate-resilient catchment planning.

Research Tools

Climate–Phenology Sensitivity Model (CPSM)

The Climate–Phenology Sensitivity Model (CPSM) is designed to quantify and analyze the sensitivity of vegetation phenology to climate variables such as temperature, precipitation, and radiation. It integrates remote sensing datasets with climate information to identify vegetation response thresholds and early warning signals of ecosystem change.

VeMATool

VeMATool (Vegetation Monitoring and Analytical Tool) is a web-based application developed on Google Earth Engine to monitor vegetation phenology, climate interactions, and land cover dynamics. It provides open access to analytical functions for vegetation-climate research, enabling real-time insights into ecosystem processes.
